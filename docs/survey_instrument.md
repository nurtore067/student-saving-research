# Proposed survey instrument (draft; pilot before real use)

**Eligibility/ethics:** 18-24 years old, university student in Almaty,
voluntary informed consent. Use anonymous IDs. Explain that answers do not
change real borrowing or credit; never require bank data.

**Baseline, before randomization:**
1. How achievable is your current saving goal? (1-7)
2. How likely are you to put money toward this goal in the next month? (1-7)
3. Your age (18-24); current employment status.
4. Approximate money left each month after essential spending (KZT range).
5. Family financial support (optional monthly KZT range).
6. Do you currently have an installment payment obligation? (yes/no)
7. A non-sensitive saving goal category and approximate target amount (optional).

**Random assignment to condition:**
- A: full total visually prominent; 4 x 6,000 KZT also shown; generic guidance.
- B: 4 x 6,000 KZT visually prominent; total 24,000 KZT also shown; same generic guidance.
- C: identical B card; personalized AI-assisted saving guidance (once built and
  reviewed). It must not modify product cost or fees.

**Post-exposure, same exact wording across all arms:**
8. How achievable is your saving goal? (1-7)
9. How likely are you to put money toward this goal in the next month? (1-7)
10. How likely are you to buy these headphones at the shown terms? (1-7)
11. What is the full amount you would pay for these headphones? (KZT number)
12. Optional: Was any information confusing? (text for pilot only)

**Consent handling:** participants without consent are excluded. Predefine the
handling of missing post scores before running the real study; the demo excludes
incomplete rows and reports the exclusion rate. Avoid public release of raw
participant responses even when IDs are anonymous if re-identification is
possible from auxiliary data.
